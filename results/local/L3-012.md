# L3-012: cross-release OpenSearch 2/3 restore stalls (~17 s at p8, ~44 s at p1): traced on grokbox, not reproduced

## What kvmsvr1 showed (from its relcmp data, analysed in L3-010 / HANDOFF)

- Only OpenSearch 2 and 3; never OS 1.3 or Elasticsearch.
- They add a fixed amount to one run: about +5-8 s on restore-edge cases, +12 s at p8 and +18-22 s at p1.
- They hit every tool: Node, 1.1.0 and 1.2.0. For example, os3-secure Node p8 20.3 s vs 8.5 s; os3-plain Node p1
  54.1 s vs 40.2 s; os3-secure 1.1.0 p8 17.1 s in three reps.
- 10 outliers in 8,896 runs, using the rule "> 5 s and > 1.5x the case median".
- Restores on kvmsvr1 are about 3x slower than on grokbox in general.

So this is not a searchdump-rs regression. The open question is only what the engine or host waits on.

## Tracing on grokbox (this item)

`scripts/perf/stall_loop.py` sends every request through `scripts/perf/trace_proxy.py`. For each run it records the
wall time, the 5 slowest requests, time by request kind, and the change in GC, refresh, flush, merge, write
rejections and pending tasks.

Setup:
- Engines: OS 3.9.0 and OS 2 plain, started with `scripts/it/up.sh opensearch-plain` (768m heap, 2 CPUs; the box
  had only about 2.6 GB free).
- Data: 16 x 6,250 docs, seeded by `ab_os3g.py`.
- Tool: 1.2.0 `searchdump-multi --direction=load` at p1 and p8, 15 reps each.
- Target indexes are deleted, and confirmed gone, before every run.

| engine | parallel | runs | median wall s | min | max | exit != 0 | slowest single request |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| OS 3.9.0 | 1 | 15 | 5.81 | 5.53 | 6.27 | 0 | 156 ms `_bulk` |
| OS 3.9.0 | 8 | 15 | 1.21 | 1.10 | 1.36 | 0 | 392 ms `_bulk` |
| OS 2 | 1 | 15 | 5.92 | 5.60 | 7.01 | 0 | 151 ms `_bulk` |
| OS 2 | 8 | 15 | 1.14 | 0.96 | 1.83 | 0 | 653 ms `_bulk` |

Raw data: `results/local/L3-012/stalls-os{2,3}.json`.

At p1, the time goes to 64 `_bulk` requests (about 3.2 s), 32 `_refresh` (0.36 s) and the remaining calls (under
0.15 s). Old GC never ran, write rejections were 0 and pending tasks were 0.

## Result

- **No stall in 60 traced restores.** The worst run is 1.6x its median (OS2 p8, 1.83 s, from one 653 ms bulk). That
  is far below the 12-22 s pauses seen on kvmsvr1.
- With the earlier grokbox evidence, that makes 0 stalls on grokbox: the two os3-plain Suite A passes (L3-002 and
  L3-007) plus these 60 runs.
- The pattern on kvmsvr1 points to the engine or host there:
  - the stalls hit every tool, OS 2/3 only, and add a fixed amount;
  - kvmsvr1 restores are 3x slower in general.
  The likely candidates are a slow fsync or translog sync on index creation or the first refresh, or an
  OS 2/3 background task (for example the 2.x+ segment-replication or remote-store checks, or a JVM safepoint
  under memory pressure). This is a hypothesis, not measured.
