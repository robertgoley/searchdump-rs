# L-808r: shared batches through the Sink (no per-batch copy at --concurrency > 1)

Owner approval 2026-11-14 (t86u). Behaviour unchanged.

## Change
- `Sink::write_all` and `BulkSink::write_batch` take the batch as `bytes::Bytes` instead of `&[u8]`.
- The file-restore loop (`pipeline::file_to_sink`) and the dump/copy output task (`retrieval`) turn the batch `Vec`
  into `Bytes` (no copy) and pass the `--offset`/`--size` window as a zero-copy `slice_ref`.
- `BulkSink::write_batch` with `--concurrency` > 1 moves that `Bytes` into the worker task; it used to
  `Bytes::copy_from_slice` every batch. File sinks still borrow the bytes.

## Measurements
100,000-doc NDJSON restore into the local ES 8.19 (1 shard, 0 replicas), `--limit 5000 --noRefresh --quiet`,
`getrusage` of the child; L-808q `f875663` vs L-808r interleaved, 9 runs for CPU, 3 for RSS (medians; the host
had noisy first runs).

| Build | `--concurrency` | CPU s | wall s | peak RSS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-808q | 4 | 0.41 | 1.20 | ~251 MB |
| L-808r | 4 | 0.37 | 1.21 | ~244 MB |
| M6 `704641c` | 4 | 0.38 | 1.25 | ~111 MB |
| L-808q | 1 | 0.35 | 3.37 | ~128 MB |
| L-808r | 1 | 0.36 | 3.46 | ~118 MB |
| M6 `704641c` | 1 | 0.38 | 3.37 | ~49 MB |

CPU is level with M6 at both settings (the L-808q 0.355 s figure was measured in a quieter session; the same-session
pair above is the comparison). Removing the copy saves about 3% peak RSS at `--concurrency 4` and about 8% at 1
(fewer short-lived allocations). The gap to M6 memory (about 2.2x at 4, 2.4x at 1) is not the batch copy: it scales
with in-flight batches, so the next suspects are the 4-batch file-reader channel, the 1.25x encoded buffer kept per
in-flight batch, and allocator retention (mimalloc); not changed here.
