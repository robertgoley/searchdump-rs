Reading:
- The engine is bimodal: in reps 0-1 the parent breaker tripped 3-5 times per run, in reps 2-3 it did not trip at all
  for the two variants without `retryfirst` (4.7-4.9 s). Four reps are too few for a precise ratio; the medians mix
  both modes.
- The floor did what it is for: after splits the cap stayed at 1024 KB instead of 217/391 KB, and the floor runs
  were as fast or faster than 1.2.0 in every rep (11.1 vs 15.4 s and 14.6 vs 13.9 s with trips, equal without).
  No failures, all 20,000 documents in every run.
- `retryfirst` drew a breaker trip in every rep (2-4), even the reps where the others had none: resending the same
  size right after a trip re-trips while the heap is still full. Unlike L3-011 (6.7 s for rf+gb), it was the slowest
  here. More data on the 429 policy (decision 2) is in L3-016/L3-017.
